## 6. NOTES AND WORKING NOTES

### Note 1 — Sept 6 Cash Deposit
The Sh 310,000 deposited into the bank on Sept 6 is an internal transfer (contra entry):
- **Dr Bank 310,000 / Cr Cash 310,000**
- This does NOT affect total wealth, only the split between Cash and Bank.

### Note 2 — Ben's Account
Ben owed Sh 110,000. He returned goods worth Sh 4,000 and paid Sh 96,000 by cheque.
- Outstanding balance = 110,000 − 4,000 − 96,000 = **Sh 10,000** (still owed)

### Note 3 — Charles's Account
Charles supplied goods worth Sh 75,000 on credit. Megan paid Sh 70,000 by cheque.
- Outstanding balance = 75,000 − 70,000 = **Sh 5,000** (still owed to Charles)

### Note 4 — Returns Inwards
Returns Inwards (Sales Returns) reduce revenue. Ben returned Sh 4,000 worth of goods.

### Note 5 — Motor Vehicle
The pickup bought for Sh 400,000 is a **fixed asset** and appears in the Trial Balance as a Debit.
